Z-score model

The original model, ported faithfully from the 2020 TensorFlow Probability version (kept in legacy/ as the reference; scripts/legacy_parity.jl checks the port).

z = competitor effect + machine-season effect + noise,   noise ~ N(0, σ_y)
  • Outcome: each row's time standardised within its stage (standardise_times!: population sd, as the legacy sklearn pipeline did).
  • Rows: only rows with a time. In F1 that means lead-lap finishers only, which silently drops the slower half of most fields. The gap models fix this.
  • Effects: non-centred, competitor effects sum to zero, no intercept by default (z has mean exactly 0 within every stage). σ_y is fixed at 1 as in the legacy model; σ_y = nothing learns it.
  • Speed: the likelihood is computed from per-(driver, car-season) sufficient statistics, so a full F1 fit takes under 2 minutes on a Raspberry Pi 5.
julia> d = prepare(fetch_results(ErgastCSV(FIXTURE), 2019))
ModelData(17 obs, 12 competitors, 8 machine-seasons)
chain = fit_effects(d; n_chains = 4)
convergence_summary(chain)          # worst R-hat and bulk ESS
eff = effects_table(chain, d)       # (; competitors, machines), in z units

See crossed_effects for every change from the legacy model and the reasons for it.
