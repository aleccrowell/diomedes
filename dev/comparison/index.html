Model comparison

The pace models are compared by expected log predictive density (elpd) on the same rows: every timed and lapped row of the data.

PSIS-LOO

pointwise_loglik recomputes each row's log-likelihood for every posterior draw. Turing can't do this itself, because the likelihood is added in one fused call. The result is shaped (draws, chains, rows) for PosteriorStats' loo. Models are named as in model_spec, so a model's terms are read from its name:

ll = pointwise_loglik(chain, g, "pl_dur_rw_kappa_age_big_slope_dev")

scripts/loo_compare.jl compares every fitted model with saved chains.

Grouped K-fold

PSIS-LOO is unreliable for rows with Pareto k > 0.7: about 3% of rows, mostly extreme results, and more as the models grow. K-fold refits the model without each fold and scores the held-out rows exactly.

  • kfold_folds deals each race's rows round-robin into K folds, so each race keeps most of its rows in training and its intercept stays determined.
  • subset_gaps gives the training data. Its indices line up with the full data.
  • heldout_loglik scores the held-out rows, and elpd_rows turns log-likelihood draws into per-row elpd.

scripts/kfold.jl runs folds as independent processes and compares models with paired differences over the same folds.
