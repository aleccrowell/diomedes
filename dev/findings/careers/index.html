Careers

The shape of a career

The population age curve: how pace changes with age for the average driver, in % of race time (negative = faster), with a 90% band. Only its shape is identified, not its overall tilt or the age of its minimum (see The question and what is identifiable). Read the bends, not the slope.

The vertical axis is reversed, so faster is higher. Ages above about 42 have few results, which is why the band widens there.

Individual trajectories

On top of the shared curve, each driver has a linear career slope: how much faster or slower they got per decade of age relative to the average driver's trajectory. Negative means they improved more than the average driver did. The 15 largest in each direction, among drivers with at least 40 results:

Intervals are ±1.645 sd (about 90%). Kubica's decline is his comeback after a serious injury.
