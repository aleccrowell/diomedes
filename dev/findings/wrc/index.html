Rallying (WRC)

The pace + loss model fitted to World Rally Championship stage times, 2018–2025, top two tiers (Rally1, including WRC cars, and Rally2, including R5). Each special stage is a "race". Cars are grouped by manufacturer × tier × season. See Data handling for how rally data differs from F1.

Relative to whom?

Driver effects are relative to the average of the … drivers in the data, most of them amateurs in Rally2 cars. That's why the fastest driver sits …% from the average. Stage results are much noisier than F1 races, and the model has no road-order or surface terms (#33).

Cars by season

Manufacturer × tier effects within each season (negative = faster). Rally1 cars are about 4–5% faster than Rally2 cars, and the model separates the tiers' car advantage from their drivers'.

Levels with at least 50 stage times. The vertical axis is reversed, so faster is higher.
