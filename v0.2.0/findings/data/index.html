Data downloads

Every chart on these pages is drawn from the CSV files below: posterior summaries exported from named fits by scripts/export_site.jl. Each directory has a manifest.json recording which fit the tables came from, its data, its convergence, and when and from which commit they were exported. Effects in % are on the log scale 100·log(T / T_ref); negative is faster. Intervals are 90% posterior intervals (q05, q95). sd is the posterior standard deviation.

Race-model files

F1 1950–2019, best pace + loss model. manifest.json

FileOne row perColumns
drivers.csvdriver id, name; rows: results used; first_season, last_season; mean, sd, q05, q95: driver effect, % of race time in average-season units (κ = 1), relative to the average driver; slope_mean, slope_sd: career slope, % per decade of age, relative to the average driver's trajectory
cars.csvcar-season id (constructor_season), constructor, season, rows; mean, sd, q05, q95: car effect, % of race time relative to the other cars in the same season; dev_mean, dev_sd: in-season development trend, % from the season's first round to its last, relative to the field
seasons.csvseason season, races; pi_*: probability that a result includes a routine loss; lambda_*: mean routine loss, % of race time (both at average race duration); kappa_*: pace scale κ
age_curve.csvage (years) age, rows; mean, q05, q95: population career curve, % of race time. Only its shape is identified: it has no constant or linear part

Retirement-model files

F1 1950–2019, competing-risks hazards. Effects are on the log-hazard scale: +0.5 is a rate 1.65× the average. manifest.json

FileOne row perColumns
cars.csvcar-season id, constructor, season; effect, sd: mechanical-failure log-hazard relative to the season; starts, mech_dnf: starts and mechanical retirements
drivers.csvdriver id, name; effect, sd: incident-proneness log-hazard relative to the average driver; starts, incidents, collisions
seasons.csvseason mech, incident, collision: modelled retirements per start over a race distance, by cause; *_obs: observed rates

Lap-model files

F1 2011–2019, clean laps, recorded pit stops. Effects are % of the race's median clean lap. manifest.json

FileOne row perColumns
drivers.csvdriver id, name, mean, sd, laps
cars.csvcar-season id, mean, sd, laps
vs_race_drivers.csvdriver in both models lap_mean, lap_sd: lap model; race_mean, race_sd: race model (whole career)
vs_race_cars.csvcar-season in both models lap_mean, lap_sd, race_mean, race_sd, constructor, season, laps

WRC files

WRC 2018–2025 stage times, top two tiers. Effects are % of stage time. manifest.json

FileOne row perColumns
drivers.csvdriver id (wrc.com id), name; mean, sd, q05, q95: relative to the average of all 641 drivers; stages
cars.csvmanufacturer × tier × season id, manufacturer, tier, season; mean, sd, q05, q95: within season; stage_times
